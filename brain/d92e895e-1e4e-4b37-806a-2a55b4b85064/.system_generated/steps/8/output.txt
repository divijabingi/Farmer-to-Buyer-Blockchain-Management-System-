### Brief Breakdown of the States:
* **Placed:** The customer has successfully submitted and paid for the order, and it is awaiting fulfillment/processing.
* **Shipped:** The order has left the warehouse and is currently in transit with a logistics/delivery carrier.
* **Delivered:** The carrier has successfully dropped off the package at the destination address.
* **Completed:** The transaction is fully closed, usually after a return window has passed or the customer has explicitly finalized the order.
* **Disputed:** An issue has been raised by the customer (e.g., damaged goods, missing items, or payment chargebacks), pausing normal lifecycle completion until resolved.
