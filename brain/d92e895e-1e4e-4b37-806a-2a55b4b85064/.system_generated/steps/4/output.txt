The search for ""Farmer marks Shipped. Buyer confirms Delivered, then funds go to the farmer"" returned the following summary:
This describes an **escrow payment workflow** typically used in online marketplaces, agricultural e-commerce platforms, or peer-to-peer (P2P) direct trade apps connecting farmers directly with buyers[1][2]. 

Here is how this process works and why it is beneficial for both parties:

### **The Step-by-Step Workflow**
1. **Order & Escrow:** The buyer purchases produce (e.g., fresh crops, grains, or farm goods) through a platform. The buyer’s payment is not sent directly to the farmer immediately; instead, it is held securely in an **escrow account** managed by the platform or payment gateway.
2. **Farmer Marks Shipped:** Once the farmer harvests, packages, and dispatches the produce, they update the order status on the platform to **"Shipped"** (often providing tracking details or proof of dispatch)[1].
3. **Buyer Confirms Delivered:** When the shipment arrives, the buyer inspects the produce (sometimes via an "Open Box Delivery" policy to check quality and quantity) and confirms receipt by marking the order as **"Delivered"** or "Accepted" on the platform[1].
4. **Funds Released:** Once the buyer’s confirmation is logged, the platform automatically releases the funds from the escrow account and transfers them to the farmer's linked bank account or digital wallet.

---

### **Why This System is Used (Benefits)**
* **Trust & Security for Buyers:** Buyers are protected against fraud, non-delivery, or receiving spoiled/substandard produce. They know their money won't be handed over until they actually receive what they paid for[1].
* **Payment Security for Farmers:** Farmers are protected from scam buyers who claim they never received items or refuse to pay after delivery. Because the buyer’s funds are already deposited into escrow *before* shipping begins, the farmer has a guarantee that the money is real and waiting for them upon successful delivery.
* **Dispute Resolution:** If a problem arises (e.g., the package is lost, delayed, or damaged), the platform can step in to mediate refunds or replacements before releasing the held funds.
